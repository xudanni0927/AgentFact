# Dataset Usage Policy

## Purpose

This dataset is intended for academic research, educational use, and non-commercial experimentation in misinformation detection and fact-checking research.

## Data Sources

Some metadata and annotations are derived from publicly accessible fact-checking content published by Snopes and other sources.

Original articles, images, and media remain the property of their respective copyright holders.

## Redistribution

This repository does not redistribute full copyrighted articles unless explicitly permitted.

Users are responsible for retrieving original content from the corresponding source websites and complying with their terms of service.

## Restrictions

Users may not use this dataset for:

* harassment or abuse
* automated misinformation generation
* surveillance targeting individuals
* commercial resale
